How does lemonic integrate Web Dev, AI, and Cybersecurity?

Traditional companies hire four disconnected agencies: one for code, one for marketing, one for automation, and one for security. lemonic brings them under a unified sprint model. Your web and mobile apps are built lightning-fast with modern frameworks, hooked into autonomous AI workflows, fueled by high-ROAS Meta and UGC ads, and protected by rigorous VAPT audits and 24/7 SOC monitoring.

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) alongside SEO?

While standard SEO ranks your site on Google results pages, AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) positions your brand as the primary reference and cited authority inside AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. We combine deep technical SEO, structured schema data, and entity authority so your brand wins traditional search algorithms and generative AI models alike.

How do your Meta Ads and UGC pipelines scale profitable ROAS?

We don't just buy ad space; we build high-converting creative engines. Our team scripts, films, and edits high-velocity User Generated Content (UGC) crafted specifically for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Facebook feeds. Combined with granular Meta audience testing, server-side Conversion API (CAPI) tracking, and dedicated conversion-rate-optimized landing pages, we scale customer acquisition profitably.

What is included in your VAPT & 24/7 SOC security operations?

Our security division provides dual-layer offensive and defensive cybersecurity. Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) simulates real-world hacker attacks across your web apps, mobile apps, APIs, and cloud infrastructure to eliminate zero-day vulnerabilities. Our 24/7 Security Operations Center (SOC) provides continuous telemetry monitoring, anomaly detection, incident response, and regulatory compliance readiness (ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR).
